What guides us

“What we build is only the way through.”

What we build is not the destination. A tool, model, method, or system is a structure that helps us cross from uncertainty toward understanding—and from intention toward evidence.

That perspective shapes how Marcozza approaches both products and advisory work: make intent explicit, test assumptions, examine what actually happened, and remain willing to improve the structure when it no longer serves the journey.

Marcozza’s work is informed by more than 25 years of hands-on experience in enterprise systems, including Azure Integration Services, Dynamics 365, middleware, software assurance, metamorphic testing, and independent acceptance verification.
