About Marcozza

Marcozza Inc. is an independent software company developing cloud-based products for designing, understanding, and validating complex technology systems. Its first product is an early-stage visual modeling and validation platform for teams building AI-powered systems.

Marcozza’s product development is informed by more than 25 years of hands-on experience in enterprise systems, including Azure Integration Services, Dynamics 365, middleware, software assurance, metamorphic testing, and independent acceptance verification.

In addition to developing proprietary software, Marcozza may provide selective technical advisory services in enterprise integration, AI assurance, and buyer-side software verification.
