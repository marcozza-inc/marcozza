Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Marcozza Inc., a Québec company, collects, uses, shares and keeps personal information in connection with marcozza.com and its services and products: consulting engagements, consultation calls, courses, cohorts and other digital products. Products with their own policy, such as Agent Modeler (agentmodeler.com), are governed by that policy.

What we collect and why

  • When you email us: your name, contact details, organisation and message, so we can reply.
  • When you book or buy: your contact details, booking and order information, and payment history, to deliver the offer, manage the relationship and meet our accounting and tax obligations. We do not receive your full card number: the payment processor handles it.
  • During an engagement, call, course or cohort: communications, information needed for the work, and your participation, to deliver the service.

We collect this information directly from you or through the platforms you use to book or buy. We use it only for the purposes stated here or with your consent.

Website and cookies

The site uses no cookies, analytics, forms or embedded third-party content. Our hosting provider may log technical data, such as IP addresses, for site security and operation.

Sharing with service providers

We share with service providers the information they need to serve us, in these categories: website hosting, email, sales, booking and course platforms, payment processing, video conferencing and accounting. We do not sell personal information.

Information processed outside Québec

Some of these providers process or store information outside Québec, including in the United States. Before information is shared outside Québec, we assess whether it will be adequately protected, as the law requires.

Retention

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 2 years.
  • Client, booking and billing records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, to meet tax obligations.
  • Course or cohort participation: while your access lasts, then up to 2 years.

After that, information is destroyed or anonymised as the law allows.

Safeguards

We protect information with measures suited to its sensitivity, including secured accounts and access limited to people who need it. Do not send credentials or sensitive information by email. Agree a suitable method with us first.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal information or, in certain cases, to receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format. You may also withdraw consent where applicable or make a complaint. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Person in charge and contact

Person in charge of protecting personal information, Marcozza Inc. · support@marcozza.com

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of the page shows the current version.
